# Machine reader artwork

Barcode controls and the dedicated machine L-mark are available only for profiles
with a barcode reader. The ordinary gutter/cut registration marks remain separate.

## Barcode

- Code 39, no optional Mod-43 checksum. Start/stop asterisks are generated by the
  encoder and are not entered in the payload.
- Exactly five ASCII digits: three for saved machine job 000–999, followed by
  quantity 00–99. Leading zeroes are preserved. Quantity 00 has no inferred
  special behavior; its meaning must follow the machine. This is independent of
  calculated pieces per sheet.
- Black bars span exactly 45 mm horizontally and 6.5 mm vertically, including
  the start/stop symbols. White quiet zones lie outside this area.
- Right edge of black bars is 52 mm from the sheet's right edge.
- Top of bars is 4–20 mm from the leading edge, inclusive. Automatic placement
  searches that band above the artwork; manual position uses millimetres.
- Fixed size and right-edge position are never relaxed to make a layout fit.
  Both artwork and the quiet zones must clear printable margins and other marks
  and finishing lines. Incompatible layouts block PDF export with an explanation.
- Legacy Code 128 or arbitrary-text payloads need review; they are not truncated
  or silently reinterpreted as machine job/quantity data.

## Dedicated registration mark

The mark is two black rectangles forming a top-right corner (┐): a horizontal
arm extends left and a vertical arm extends down. Each arm is at least 5 mm long
and at least 0.4 mm thick. Thickness must remain less than length to retain an L.
The entire mark lies within the square between 3 and 20 mm from both the top and
right paper edges. This makes 17 mm the maximum possible arm length.

Placement searches clear positions in that corner region and also respects the
configured printable area, artwork bleed, ordinary registration marks and
finishing lines. An unavailable mark is omitted with an explicit warning; no
invented guide coordinates are provided. Default arms are 5 × 0.4 mm.

The guide/INI registration offsets refer to the outer top edge and outer right
edge of the corner mark. The same rectangle geometry feeds preview and PDF export.
Barcode and mark geometry do not affect the layout engine's piece count or cuts.
